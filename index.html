Every model gets the same single request per task: the task text, its input files inline and its drawings as images, with medium reasoning effort and at most 20 000 output tokens. It must answer with one complete Python program. It gets no tools and no feedback. We re-run that program once from scratch in an offline sandbox (600 s, 6 GB, CadQuery, Gmsh, scikit-fem and CalculiX available) and score the numbers it writes with the frozen evaluator. With 100 tasks, 31 passes has a 95 % interval of about 23 to 41, so the top four are not clearly separated. No pass has been expert-reviewed yet. † Kimi-K3 and GLM-5.3 ran at low reasoning effort and DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash at none: at medium they spent the whole 20 000-token budget on reasoning and returned no program.

What counts as a pass?

Numerical accuracy

Every requested value must fall within its task-specific tolerance around the reference. Partial credit measures the fraction of values in band.

Execution evidence

The submitted code, geometry, mesh, fields and convergence record support the reported answer. Required artifacts depend on the task suite.

Expert review

A verified pass also requires review of the geometry, supports, loads and simulation workflow. Numerical success alone is reported separately.
